Deverakonda Vijay Sai (born 9 May 1989), widely known as Vijay Deverakonda,[1] is an Indian actor and film producer who works in Telugu films.[2][3] Vijay is an recipient of a Filmfare Award, a Nandi Award and three SIIMA Awards.[4] Deverakonda made his debut with Nuvvila (2011) and received praise for his supporting role in the coming-of-age drama Yevade Subramanyam (2015). He rose to prominence as a leading film actor by starring in Pelli Choopulu (2016) and Arjun Reddy (2017), winning the Filmfare Award for Best Actor – Telugu for his performance as a volatile surgeon in the latter.
